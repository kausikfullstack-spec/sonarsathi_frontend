# Illustrative catalogue photography

These locally stored product photos are selected from Chip Lee Jewellery and
Kushal's public product catalogues. `sources.json` preserves the original product
page and image URLs for each pair. The source product's alternate photograph is
used for hover and keyboard focus, rather than another design or a cropped copy.

The website remains a sample catalogue: these photographs do not verify the
fictional store listings, names, metal specifications, pricing, or availability.
The two remaining coin illustrations are supplied project assets.

Photo records are mapped to catalogue IDs in `src/data/product-images.ts`.
